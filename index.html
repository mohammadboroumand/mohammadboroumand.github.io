Job Market Paper

To Assuage or to Evade: Managerial Non-Answer and the Audience in the Room

Mohammad Boroumand & Majid Majzoubi

Using approximately 14,000 S&P 500 earnings calls, we examine why managers answer analysts in some settings but evade them in others. I develop an LLM-based classification procedure to identify managerial non-answers and combine it with analysts' questioning and coverage histories.

FindingManagers are more likely to provide non-answers when analysts are more heterogeneous and less likely to do so when analysts are more familiar with the firm. Both relationships become stronger following negative evaluations.

Strategic Positioning

Maintaining the Right Distance: A Dyadic and Dynamic View of Firm Positioning

Mohammad Boroumand, Majid Majzoubi, Kamyar Goudarzi & Eric Y. Zhao

Using transformer-based embeddings of 10-K business descriptions across more than 150,000 focal firm–rival dyads, we study how firms reposition when close rivals change their strategic position.

FindingFirms tend to follow rivals that move away and differentiate when rivals move toward them. Analyst evaluations and shared analyst coverage shape these responses.

Working paper · Finalist, SMS Best Proposal Award for Rigor.

Other Research Projects

Noise Interacts with Value to Create Innovation Premiums and Penalties

Wayne Johnson, David Tannenbaum & Mohammad Boroumand

We examine how novelty generates disagreement among evaluators and show that disagreement penalizes novelty among higher-rated options but creates an innovation premium among lower-rated options.

Working paper · Association for Consumer Research Annual Conference, 2026.

Identity Claim Changes and Analyst Coverage: The Moderating Role of Categorical Context

Mohammad Boroumand, Majid Majzoubi & Suresh Kotha

We examine how changes in firms' claimed identities affect analyst coverage and how categorical context conditions those consequences.

Invited resubmission, Organization Studies.
