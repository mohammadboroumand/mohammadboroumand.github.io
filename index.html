Job Market Paper

To Assuage or to Evade: Managerial Non-Answer and the Audience in the Room

with Majid Majzoubi

Using approximately 14,000 S&P 500 earnings calls, we examine why managers answer analysts in some settings but evade them in others. I develop an LLM-based classification procedure to identify managerial non-answers and combine it with analysts' questioning and coverage histories.

Finding Managers are more likely to provide non-answers when analysts are more heterogeneous and less likely to do so when analysts are more familiar with the firm. Both relationships become stronger following negative evaluations.

Manuscript ready for submission to Organization Science.

Strategic Positioning

Maintaining the Right Distance: A Dyadic and Dynamic View of Firm Positioning

with Majid Majzoubi, Kamyar Goudarzi, and Eric Zhao

Using transformer-based embeddings of 10-K business descriptions across more than 150,000 focal firm–rival dyads, we study how firms reposition when close rivals change their strategic position.

Finding Firms tend to follow rivals that move away and differentiate when rivals move toward them. Analyst evaluations and shared analyst coverage shape these responses.

Ready for submission to Journal of Management. Finalist, SMS Best Proposal Award for Rigor.

Other Research Projects

Noise Interacts with Value to Create Innovation Premiums and Penalties

with Wayne Johnson and David Tannenbaum

We examine how novelty generates disagreement among evaluators and show that disagreement penalizes novelty among higher-rated options but creates an innovation premium among lower-rated options.

In preparation for Organization Science. ACR 2026.
